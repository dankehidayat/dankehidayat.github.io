Advanced IoT monitoring of electrical energy and environment using ESP32, with intelligent Mamdani fuzzy classification for real-time electricity and temperature analysis.

## Highlights

- PZEM-004T power monitoring and DHT11 temperature/humidity with enhanced calibration.
- Identifies energy waste patterns in office environments through fuzzy analysis.
- Electronics schematic, firmware, Blynk dashboard, and Google Sheets as cloud database.
