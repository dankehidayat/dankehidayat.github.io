Environmental monitoring on ESP32 for temperature, humidity, atmospheric pressure, altitude, and soil moisture.

## Highlights

- Real-time LCD display, cloud remote monitoring, and precise RTC timing with SQW interrupts.
- Sensor fusion for accurate environmental collection and analysis.
- Modular sensor architecture aimed at precision agriculture and environmental research.
